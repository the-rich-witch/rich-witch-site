Why this matters

You can keep trying new money fixes and still feel like something hasn't moved.

A budget doesn't explain why you freeze up before checking your balance. A better plan doesn't touch the feeling that something's "stuck" no matter what you try. That's usually the moment people start looking past the spreadsheet — not because the practical side stopped mattering, but because it stopped being the whole picture.

Left unexamined, that gap can turn into second-guessing yourself, spending on one solution after another, or feeling split between what you believe practically and what you know spiritually.

A consultation can help you understand what's spiritually active, decide whether work is called for, and move forward with more clarity instead of fear.
