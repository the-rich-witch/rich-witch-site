IV
What "the work" can look like.Simple, focused work

Rootwork Services

Every piece of work is kept simple and focused, using common materials — no rare curios, no elaborate setups.

Petition Work

Write down what's going on and what you're asking for, in your own words. Every petition is folded and prepared using traditional methods, then either kept as a focus for prayer or burned as part of a candle working.

Cleansing Baths

A bath prepared with salt and herbs suited to what you're carrying, meant to clear away what's weighing on you before you move forward. You'll get clear instructions to prepare and take the bath yourself with supplies you source locally. Prefer it prepared and mailed to you? Add $30 for materials and shipping.

Candle Work

One candle, dressed and burned with a single clear intention tied to your petition. Straightforward, focused work — not a large altar setup, just one flame doing one job.

Sachet Powder

A small packet of herbs prepared ahead of time for common needs like protection, clarity, or peace in the home. Carry it, keep it near your bed, or add it to a bath. Prepared and mailed to you for an additional $30 for materials and shipping.
