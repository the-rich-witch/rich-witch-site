This practice came before the brand.

I've practiced hoodoo and rootwork for more than a decade.

I'm a practicing rootworker and eclectic witch — meaning I study and practice more than one tradition — with more than eleven years of practice, and I graduated from catherine yronwode's Lucky Mojo Hoodoo Rootwork Correspondence Course in 2015.

My broader practice includes cartomancy with playing cards, candle work, spiritual cleansing, planetary timing, and manifestation. I listen to what's happening, look at it through the lens of rootwork, and tell you plainly what I see and what it means.

A reading isn't about making everything mysterious. It's about looking closely at the condition and choosing work that fits.
